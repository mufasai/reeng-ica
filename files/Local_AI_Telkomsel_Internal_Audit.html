02.A · Self-Approval Risk

Segregation-of-duties failure, flagged at scale.

07 / 14

Our local AI identified a high-risk cluster of users in the ERP database who routinely bypass segregation of duties (SoD). Through heuristic and pattern analysis, the system flagged transactions that were created and approved by the same User ID within the same session.

Behavioural clustering. Groups users whose approval patterns deviate from peer baselines.
Session-level tracing. Links create-approve actions to the same authenticated session.
Threshold tuning. Audit-team adjustable to surface only material exposure.

03.B · Mathematical Precision

An SSP error humans missed — caught.

Our custom RAG pipeline detected a stand-alone selling price (SSP) allocation error inside the MSA document. The original calculation used an incorrect denominator, materially overstating contract assets.

11 / 14
Financial Impact
~40%

overstatement of contract assets prevented — by a single line of correction.

Human Error

Used CU 1,600 as denominator

Treated the smartphone-only price as the total — allocating 50% of the contract value to the device.

AI Correction

Total SSP = CU 2,720

Correct smartphone allocation is 29.41%, not 50%. Contract-asset booking restated to a defensible IFRS-15 basis.
